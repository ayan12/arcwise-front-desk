The hand behind the work

        

          
Koka is a Bali tattoo artist who started tattooing in 2023 and has been winning awards at the conventions he enters ever since.

          
His favourite is black & grey realism: tigers, lions, dragons and samurai at full-back scale. His Japanese colour work, hannya, phoenix and koi, is bold enough to win best colour.

          
He co-founded Blustereast Tattoo Studio in Bali, and now brings his chair to Jakarta.

Before you book

      

        
How much is a session?
On the Jakarta tour, Rp 8.999K per session. The first 2 people to book save Rp 1,500,000 per session. In Bali, Koka quotes each piece in DM.

        
Where is the Jakarta tour?
Green Lake City, America Cluster, Jakarta, from 3 to 13 November 2026. There are only 10 sessions.

        
What styles does Koka do?
Black & grey realism is his favourite, and he also does Japanese colour. He specialises in large work: full backs, full sleeves and full legs.

        
How many sessions does a full back take?
It depends on the design and how you sit. One Japanese full back was finished in 3 days back to back. Koka will plan it with you in DM.

        
How do I book?
Send Koka a DM on Instagram with your idea, placement and size, or let the booking assistant on this page get your message ready.
