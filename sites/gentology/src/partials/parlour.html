Why the twenties

Art Deco, jazz, and a proper haircut.

The 1920s gave us Art Deco and the birth of jazz, the decade of Duke Ellington and Charlie Chaplin, swing, foxtrot and the waltz. It was also when a gentleman's barber was a ritual rather than an errand. That is the ambience Gentology keeps, from the panelling to the records.
