Questions couples ask

Do you travel outside Bali and Jakarta?

Yes. We work across Indonesia and run scheduled trips abroad. The next ones are Japan in January 2027 and Singapore in February 2027.

What is a same-day edit?

A short film of your wedding, edited while the day is still happening and played for your guests at the reception.

How much does a package cost?

It depends on your date, location and whether you want photo, video or both. Send your details and Admin Jean will share the packages that fit. The Japan winter session is IDR 18.000.000.

How far ahead should we book?

Popular dates go first, so get in touch as soon as you have your date. We will tell you straight away whether it is open.

Can we book photo or video only?

Yes. Choose what you need in the form above and we will send the matching options.
