A note before you begin

Years from now, you won’t remember the timeline or the seating chart. You’ll remember his hand finding yours before the doors opened, your mother fixing your veil one last time, and the moment everyone you love stood up at once.

That is what we are there for.

With love, TerraNova

Chapter one

The quiet hour

Before the music, before the guests. The dress, the rings, the nerves you only admit to each other. We stay close and stay quiet, and let it be yours.

Chapter two

The vow

Every guest turns. The veil trails behind you, and for a few minutes the whole world is very small. We capture it the way it felt, not the way it was posed.

Chapter three

The celebration

Shoes off, lipstick on the glass, your best friend’s terrible speech. The loud, joyful part of the day, captured with all its chaos, then cut into a film you will play for years.

Chapter four

Just the two of you

When it all goes quiet, we steal you away for a few minutes. A walk, a look, a hand held a little tighter. Often it is the photo couples love most.

The way we work

Honest, cinematic, and never in the way

  • Photo and film, one team

    Our photographers and filmmakers work as one crew, so your pictures and your film tell the same story.

  • Real moments, gently guided

    We direct only when it helps. The rest of the time we watch for the glance, the laugh and the tear you didn’t know anyone saw.

  • Your film, the same night

    With a same-day edit, your guests watch the story of your day at the reception, while it is still happening.
