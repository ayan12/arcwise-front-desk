What we do

Tell us what you want, or show us a photo. We take the time to get it right.

  • Haircut

    Scissor or clipper cut, shaped to how you wear it.

  • Skin fade

    Low, mid or high, blended clean.

  • Hair design

    Hard parts and shaved lines.

  • Beard trim

    Lined up and shaped to suit your face.

  • Face mask

    Black, gold or red peel-off mask in the chair.

  • Kids’ cut

    Patient cuts for the little ones.

Also on the menu: hot towel facials and hair & beard colour. Ask for prices on WhatsApp.

The shop

Stone, gold and good light.

Hexagon lights, gold arched mirrors on a hand-cut stone wall and black leather chairs. Clean, bright and air-conditioned, right on Jalan Sultan Ismail.

Walk-ins welcome. Booking just saves you the wait.
