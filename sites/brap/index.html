01

How a lid
is born

Every BRAP is laid up, sanded and painted by hand. It gets loud in here. That’s the point.

  1. Step 1our favourite

    Lay-up

    Fiberglass laid into the mould, layer by layer. The most beautiful part to watch.

  2. Step 2

    Resin

    Resin soaks the weave and sets the shell hard and light.

  3. Step 3

    Sand

    Hand-sanded until the curve is right. Then once more.

  4. Step 4

    Paint

    Enamel, flake or left raw. The Unfinished stays naked on purpose.

  5. Step 5

    The pad

    The part people miss: lambskin, soft nylex and memory foam on the inside.

The
catalogue

Lids, threads and bits for the garage. Pick a size, throw it in the bag.

    500TX series: slim-profile shell in composite fiberglass, lambskin and soft nylex lining, memory foam, tubular strap with D-ring, brass snaps and rivets, around 700g. Sizes XS to XL.
