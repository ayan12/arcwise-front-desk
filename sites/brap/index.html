    How to order.

    Stock pieces ship fast. Custom lids take longer, because someone is making yours.

    1. Pick or sketch

      Choose from the catalogue, or sketch your own lid.

    2. Check out

      Pay online. Shipping is worked out for your country and box size.

    3. We make it

      Custom lids get a proof first. Then the garage gets to work.

    4. It ships

      Boxed, tracked and on its way, wherever you ride.

    At launch: cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Worldwide shipping.
